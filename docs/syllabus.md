##### **Intelligence is becoming abundant. The advantage is becoming more capable.** 

This course is built around a simple demand: see clearly, learn quickly, relate well, use AI without surrendering judgment, and make something real. 

## **COURSE DESCRIPTION** 

**MBA 606 is a five-week leadership lab for a moment when AI is changing how people learn, decide, communicate, and build.** Students will study leadership, practice it under pressure, use Gemini and Google Workspace to extend their thinking, and produce visible evidence of what they can do. Expect less lecture and more reading, making, speaking, testing, feedback, and revision. 

**The class is organized around five recurring demands of leadership:** Reality, Possibility, Community, Responsibility, and Polarity. They help us ask what is true, what could be true, who must be understood, what must be built, and which tensions cannot be solved by choosing one side. The Leadership Profile draws from major traditions represented in Northouse: traits and skills, situational and adaptive leadership, transformational and authentic leadership, servant and relational leadership, team leadership, ethics, followership, and inclusive practice. 

**By the end of the course,** students will have a Leadership Profile, a living Context File, a T-shaped Capability Map, five mission artifacts, a working build, a Life + Work Portfolio, and a 90-day development plan. 

## **COURSE PROMISE** 

##### **Know yourself. Read the moment. Build the capabilities your future requires. Learn to work with AI. Ship something you can stand behind.** 

The work builds. What you learn in Week 1 should still be useful in Week 5, and ideally long after the class ends. 

## **LEARNING OUTCOMES** 

**1.** Read leadership situations through the five course lenses and choose an approach that fits the context instead of using one preferred style everywhere. 

**2.** Strengthen self-awareness, presence, attention, emotional self-regulation, motivation, and recovery as practical conditions for leadership and learning. 

**3.** Create and calibrate a personal Leadership Profile using self-assessment, narrative evidence, live performance, feedback, and reflection. 

**4.** Map a T-shaped capability portfolio that connects domain depth, transferable leadership capabilities, personal capacity, and intelligent use of AI. 

**5.** Use Gemini and Google Workspace to move from prompting and context engineering to prototyping, workflow design, testing, and building functional tools. 

**6.** Interview, listen, reframe, tell a clear story, and use human insight to identify a problem or opportunity worth building around. 

**7.** Build, test, revise, and present something useful for a real person, team, or organization. 

**8.** Explain, defend, verify, revise, and own AI-assisted work without handing judgment or accountability to the tool.

## **PRESENCE, CAPACITY, AND SELF-REGULATION** 

**Presence and self-regulation matter.** What you know can become hard to access when you are tired, distracted, anxious, frustrated, or under pressure. We will practice the capacity to stay present, choose a response, recover, and return to the work. 

|**Capacity**|**What it means in practice**|
|---|---|
|Attention|Keep focus where you choose and notice when something else has<br>taken it.|
|Regulation|Notice your state and choose your next move instead of acting on<br>impulse.|
|Energy + Recovery|Use sleep, movement, pacing, breaks, and recovery to stay useful<br>over time.|
|Motivation|Connect curiosity, autonomy, mastery, meaning, and responsibility to<br>work worth doing.|
|Learning + Resilience|Learn from what happened, adjust, recover, and try again.|



##### **Pause long enough to choose.** 

Before a presentation, interview, hard feedback, failed build, or final demonstration: notice what is happening, name it, regulate, choose, and recover.

## **THE CAPABILITY MAP** 

**The Real Capability 30 gives the class a common language.** No one will try to develop all thirty at once. Each student will choose three to five capabilities that matter most for the next role, challenge, or season. 

**The map is T-shaped.** The vertical bar is depth in a field, industry, craft, or problem. The horizontal bar is the range that lets you use that depth across changing situations. We will also ask where AI creates useful leverage and whether your attention, energy, regulation, and learning capacity can support the work. 

**For each selected capability, we will ask four questions.** Standard: What does good require? Situation: What does this context require? Self: What in me helps or gets in the way? Rep: What will I practice next, and what evidence will show <u>growth?</u>

## **RULES FOR HUMANS LIVING AND BUILDING WITH MACHINES** 

**These are the starting rules for the class.** We will test and revise them in Week 1. The instructors have not mastered them. We need them if AI is going to increase capability instead of hiding its absence. 

**1.** Have a thought before you have a prompt. 

**2.** Use context, not magic words. 

**3.** Keep some things hard. The struggle may be where the learning lives. 

**4.** Do not outsource the brave part: the conversation, the decision, the responsibility, or the apology. 

**5.** Look up. Other people are not inputs, personas, or data points. 

**6.** Protect private, confidential, employer-owned, customer, health, financial, personnel, and student information. 

**7.** Verify consequential claims, sources, calculations, and recommendations. 

**8.** Tell the truth about what AI did. 

**9.** Stand behind your work. Explain it, defend it, revise it, and own it. 

**AI use is permitted throughout the course unless a mission explicitly requires a human-first attempt.** Students remain responsible for every artifact, decision, recommendation, and claim they submit. AI assistance does not transfer authorship, accountability, or the obligation to understand the work. 

## **VOICE, PRIVACY, AND STUDENT OWNERSHIP** 

**Personal reflection and boundaries.** Some exercises ask you to reflect honestly on your life, work, relationships, strengths, failures, and aspirations. You control what becomes public. Depth is expected; disclosure is not. You may keep personal material private and work at the level that is appropriate for you. 

- The voice agent is a reflection and rehearsal partner. It is not a therapist, authority, or grader. 

- Instructor access will focus on approved artifacts, completion signals, and work submitted for grading, not a running transcript of raw reflection. 

- Students review, edit, and approve any AI-generated profile or context summary before it is saved. 

- Students own the final Context File and portfolio. They may export, reuse, revise, or delete personal working material consistent with course and University requirements. 

- Do not place sensitive personal or employer information into an unapproved consumer or developer tool. Use fictional, public, sanitized, or student-created data for demonstrations unless the instructors approve another arrangement.
